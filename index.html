              About roughMaps

              roughMaps turns any place into a hand-drawn map, right in your browser. Search a place, pick one of 48 looks, frame it, and download it as a PNG, SVG or PDF.

              It's free, with no ads and no sign-up. It's still in beta, so things may change or break.

              The drawing is done with Rough.js and the map with MapLibre GL. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, tiles by OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles, countries from Natural Earth.
